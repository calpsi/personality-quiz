## Kenney Nature Kit
- Creator: Kenney
- Official source: https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit
- Download mirror used: https://hydecogames.com/art/models/data/packs/kenney-nature-kit.zip
- Licence: Creative Commons CC0 (public domain dedication), confirmed on the creator's page. The downloaded licence is retained in `assets/kenney-nature/EZ-CC0-LICENSE.txt`.

## Poly Haven — Wood Table
- Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_table
- Download: https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/wood_table/wood_table_diff_1k.jpg
- Licence: CC0. https://polyhaven.com/license
- Uses: 1K diffuse wood grain, tinted and softened in the walnut material.
- Powered by Poly Haven (the public API was used to locate the download).

All downloaded material resources are packed into the Blender file. Original downloads are retained in `assets/`. Architecture, cases, low-poly specimen replicas, lighting, cameras, lettering, and decorative joinery were generated for this project. User reference images informed the style; they are not used as textures or redistributed in the scene.

Specimens are stylised artistic replicas, not scientifically accurate species reconstructions.
